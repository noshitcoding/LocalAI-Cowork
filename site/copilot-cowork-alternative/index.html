V2 evaluation guide // Updated 9 November 2026

Considering an open-source Copilot Cowork alternative

Release channels: V2 2.0.0-alpha.8 is being prepared and is not published yet. The existing public V1 release is v0.3.1. These are separate applications and data profiles. Read the V2 preview scope.

Match the integration to the work

LocalAI Cowork is an independent Apache-2.0 workspace. Its V2 preview focuses on model choice, selected files, tools, execution plans, review forms and reusable workflows. It does not include a Microsoft 365 license or automatically gain access to your tenant's applications and data.

Questions for your evaluation

  • Are the required files local, on your own server, or inside Microsoft 365?
  • Do the exact tools you configure support the intended Office, email or calendar operation?
  • Which service receives the request, and which account authorizes it?
  • Is the operation tested in the actual preview package and recoverable after a restart?
  • Do you require managed tenant governance and commercial support?

Preview scope and costs

The app has no purchase price; hardware, optional providers and connected services can have separate costs. Managed runtimes, Windows/Office automation and enterprise operation need their own setup and acceptance. They are not complete desktop preview promises.

For current Microsoft plans and functionality, consult the official Microsoft Copilot Cowork page.
